## Backlog

### FAQ: "Is hi-key free?" vs "How do credits work?"

- **Current:** Question is "Is hi-key free?" with answer opening "Practically no, but you can definitely try it for free..."
- **Variant:** Change question to "How do credits work?" and lead the answer with value: "Each credit generates 4 images from one prompt. You get 5 credits to start, free. Refer a friend and you both get 5 more. When you need more, one-time packs start at $2.99 and subscriptions at $4.99/month."
- **Hypothesis:** The current version has strong brand personality (unexpected honesty), but the "no" opener might reduce conversions. The variant is safer but loses character.
- **What to measure:** Waitlist signup rate, scroll depth past FAQ, time on page.
- **Decision (2026-03-27):** Keep current version for launch. The honesty fits the brand and anyone reading FAQ #5 is already engaged. Revisit with data.
